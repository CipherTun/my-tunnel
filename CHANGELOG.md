# Changelog

## 1.0.0+1 — clean-room foundation

- New Flutter frontend and state controller.
- Official libXray integration scaffold and Android `VpnService` TUN lifecycle.

- Secure profile/settings storage, import/export, subscriptions, QR display, diagnostics, logs, failover groups, Xray proxy chains, and split-ABI CI workflow.
